# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective project partners, clients, recruiters, and community collaborators visit the public portfolio to understand Yoga Agustiansyah's work, role, and experience quickly. The owner uses a separate CMS to maintain published content.

## Product Purpose

Show a credible body of software work and make it easy to inspect projects, experience, research, and writing before starting a conversation. The portfolio should be memorable without slowing the visitor's path to the work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, inventive, and capable. The public experience can feel playful through composition and interaction while its claims, project roles, and writing remain precise.

## Anti-references

- A research journal or OJS-style information layout for the main portfolio.
- Generic AI landing page effects, repetitive section reveals, loud multicolor gradients, and animation without a clear purpose.
- An interface that hides key content or actions behind motion, hover, or decorative elements.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with real work and clearly state Yoga's contribution.
2. Give each section a distinct rhythm while preserving one visual voice.
3. Use motion to guide attention, explain spatial changes, and acknowledge actions.
4. Keep collaboration routes and case studies accessible on every device.
5. Let CMS data change without breaking the layout or visual hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, meaningful image text, touch targets, and complete content without animation. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the responsive layout usable from mobile through desktop.
